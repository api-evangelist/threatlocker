In the criminal sphere of ransomware, Akira is known for its abundance of victims and utilizing its opportunism to achieve its goals. The Threat Intelligence team has captured samples of both the Armillaria loader and the EDR killer used by Akira, and this analysis aims to highlight the tactics leveraged to successfully terminate endpoint security vendor products.
